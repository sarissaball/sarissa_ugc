About

A creator, a storyteller and a lover of the little things that make life feel exciting.

Who I am

I'm Sarissa, a creator, storyteller and lover of beauty, fashion and all the little things that make life feel exciting. Curious by nature, a little obsessed with details, and always excited to turn ideas into something real.

Why I create

I love creating content that feels natural, looks beautiful and actually feels like a person is behind it. A good video is a small story: a mood, a detail, a reason to stay until the end.

How I see things

I'm drawn to the little details, the right mood and the story that makes a product worth discovering. Colour, texture and light decide how something feels before a word is said.

What I bring to brands

A real personality on camera, a creative eye, and the ability to adapt my universe to yours, so your product looks at home in my content instead of feeling like an ad.
